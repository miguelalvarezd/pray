# Pray

A personal prayer companion: a daily checklist of prayers and activities, a list of intentions to pray for, novenas, and the record of what was prayed on each day.

## Language

### Time

**Day**:
The unit every record is kept against. A Day runs from 04:00 to 03:59 local time, so praying at 01:00 counts toward the previous Day.
_Avoid_: calendar date (unless you really mean midnight-to-midnight), logical date

**Liturgical Day**:
The Church calendar for a Day: its season, celebrations, liturgical color and readings. It follows the Day, so at 01:00 on Sunday the app still shows Saturday's liturgy.

**Daily Log**:
The record of everything prayed and done on one Day.
_Avoid_: entry, log entry

### What is prayed

**Daily Prayer**:
One of the fixed items on the daily checklist (Morning Prayer, Holy Mass, Rosary, Evening Prayer, Bible Reading). Mass is a Daily Prayer even though it is not a prayer in the strict sense.
_Avoid_: standard prayer, prayer (bare)

**Prayer Text**:
The words to be recited, such as a novena's prayer.
_Avoid_: prayer (bare)

**Activity**:
An optional spiritual, sacramental, devotional or service practice done on a Day, such as Confession, Adoration or Volunteering.
_Avoid_: extra activity, other prayer

**Activity Category**:
The group an Activity belongs to: Sacramental, Devotional, Service or Spiritual.

**Prayer Guide**:
A step-by-step walkthrough of a Daily Prayer, such as the Rosary or Mass. Finishing it marks that Daily Prayer as done for the Day.

**Reflection**:
The short quote or verse shown for the Day.
_Avoid_: devotion

**Gratitude Entry**:
The single free-text note written on a Day. It is framed as gratitude, but the user may write anything.
_Avoid_: journal

### Intentions

**Intention**:
Something or someone the user prays for over a period of time.

**Priority**:
How important the user says an Intention is: low, medium or high.

**Target Date**:
The optional Day by which an Intention matters, such as the day of a surgery or an exam. Once it has passed, the user is asked whether the Intention was Answered.

**Urgency**:
The order in which active Intentions are shown, always derived from Priority, Target Date and age, never set by the user.

**Answered**:
An Intention the user considers granted. It records the Day it was answered and an optional note on how. It is no longer prayed for, and it can be restored.

**Archived**:
An Intention the user has stopped praying for without it being Answered. It can be restored.

### Novenas

**Novena**:
A devotion prayed on nine consecutive Days, counted from its start Day. Missing a Day does not shift the Days that follow. A Novena may be offered for an Intention (a petition) or for none (e.g. honoring a saint). Praying a Novena on a Day also counts as praying for its Intention on that Day.

**Novena Template**:
A library entry a Novena can be started from. Its Prayer Text is copied into the Novena at start, so later changes to the template never alter a Novena in progress.
_Avoid_: library novena, preset

**Completed Novena**:
A Novena that was prayed on each of its nine Days.

**Incomplete Novena**:
A Novena whose nine Days have passed with at least one Day not prayed.

### Streaks

**Prayed Day**:
A Day on which at least one Daily Prayer, Activity or Novena was prayed. Intentions and Gratitude Entries on their own do not make a Prayed Day.

**Streak**:
The number of consecutive Prayed Days ending today, or ending yesterday if today is not a Prayed Day yet.
